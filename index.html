Entry No. 01 · From my heart

To the girl behind these pages...

I made this little space because some feelings deserve more than a message on a screen. I wanted you to have somewhere you can return to whenever you want to feel close to me.

There is no perfect way to put everything I feel into words. But if you turn these pages, I hope you find little reminders of how special you are to me.

Always yours. ❤️
Entry No. 02 · The little things

Things I Adore About You

I adore your eyes, your smile, and the little things you do without even realising how much they mean to me. I love the way you make ordinary moments feel special.

But above all, I adore you for being yourself. You don't have to be perfect to be beautiful in my eyes. There is something about you that makes my heart choose you over and over again.

My favourite person. ❤️
Entry No. 03 · Between us

Things I Have Never Told You

Sometimes I look at you and wonder how someone can become so important to me. I don't always find the right words, but you mean more to me than I probably express.

I notice the little things. I remember our conversations. Even the simplest moments with you become memories I want to keep. You have no idea how often you make me smile.

Some feelings are bigger than words. ❤️
Entry No. 04 · For your difficult days

A Letter for Your Difficult Days

My love, if you're reading this on a difficult day, please remember that you are doing better than you think. You don't have to have everything figured out.

You are allowed to feel tired, to make mistakes, and to have moments when you doubt yourself. None of those things make you less beautiful or less deserving of happiness.

Please never be too hard on yourself. I hope you learn to see yourself with the same kindness and admiration that I see you with.

I'm always cheering for you. ❤️
Entry No. 05 · A promise

One Promise

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I promise to keep choosing honesty, patience and understanding.

I promise to keep learning how to love you better, to listen when you need someone to hear you, and to celebrate your victories, no matter how small they may seem.

I want to grow with you, create beautiful memories with you, and never let you forget how much you matter to me.

With all my heart. ❤️
